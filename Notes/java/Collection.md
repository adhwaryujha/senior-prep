Collections:

Ordered collections: these prioritize sequence.
    Basically this means that the collections strictly maintain the order of the element insertion. Fort this they maintain extra memory or CPU cycles to maintain the order.
    Example are arrayList, linkedList.
    Use cases: to maintain users cart in the ordr they add item, show product list sorted according to the price, name etc (sorted tree map)

Un-ordered collections: these prioritize speed.
    Basically they dont follow or maintain the order of there insertion, the insertion of elements are done based on bitwise calculations and scatred across the memory.
    Example are hashmap, hashset. As studied earlier hashmap insert values based on the hashcode of their keys.
